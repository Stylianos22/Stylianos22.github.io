IT Service Desk Automation

DAF Trucks · Business Analyst & client contact

Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

The problem

DAF's internal IT Service Desk was overloaded and many calls went unanswered. Before proposing anything, we had to find out why.

What I did

I was my team's contact with DAF and handled the meetings, emails and follow-up. After mapping how support requests flowed, I proposed one starting point for every employee: a Digital Support Hub. The AI assistant comes first and gives step-by-step help. A ticket is only created if the assistant can't solve the problem. Employees can also follow their tickets and search common fixes themselves. I backed the design with a business case (ROI and break-even) and KPIs agreed with DAF.

Outcome

DAF validated the new support flow, and the project was graded Excellent.

Store Performance Reporting

Plainwear · Business Analyst & Scrum Master

Feb – Jun 2026

The problem

Plainwear's CFO followed store performance through a manual Excel report that took months to put together.

What I did

As Business Analyst and Scrum Master, I mapped how the report was made and ranked the requirements with Plainwear. One key choice was a like-for-like view: a store is only compared with last year if a fair comparison is possible, and every excluded store shows the reason. On one screen the CFO sees the best and worst stores, revenue per product group, and this year against last year, filtered by country, month, product group or brand.

Outcome

A Power BI dashboard replaced the manual Excel report. Figures are blurred here to protect client data.

AI Test-Data Generator

Amexio · GenAI Architect

Sep 2026 – now

The problem

Amexio needs large sets of realistic documents to test document management systems, without using anyone's real data.

What I did

I'm one of two GenAI Architects in a team of six. I turned the client's answers into a clear scope and strict data rules: fully fake data, marked "AI-generated fake data", no real names and never on production servers. Our design splits the work. The AI writes the text of each document and leaves placeholders. Code then fills in every name, date and amount from one shared case file. That keeps all documents in a case consistent, like the accident statement, claim form and decision letter of one car insurance claim.

Outcome

In progress. The first milestone is one complete, realistic case, with the number of documents set in a configuration file.

Invoice Automation

ASML scenario · Business Analyst

Feb – Jun 2026

The problem

In this case, ASML's supplier invoices were handled on paper.

What I did

I modelled the new process in BPMN, a standard notation for process diagrams. Paper invoices are scanned and read automatically. Each invoice is checked against the purchase order and the goods receipt (a three-way match). A full match is approved and added to the payment batch without anyone touching it. Only exceptions reach a person: a junior employee reviews mismatches, and a senior employee handles disputes. Invoices without a purchase order number go back to the supplier.

Outcome

A design aimed at 85% of invoices processed fully automatically, so people only handle the exceptions.
